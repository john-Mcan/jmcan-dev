## Context

An IPTV playlist can hold hundreds of channels in a single file, and general-purpose players show it
as a long list that is hard to browse.

## What it is

A player for Windows: paste the address of an IPTV playlist and get the channels sorted by category,
with search, favorites, full screen and a floating window. If the signal drops, it reconnects on its
own.

## What I did

Everything: interface and development. It is named after the chucao, a bird from the forests of
southern Chile.

## Contribute

The code is published on [GitHub](https://github.com/john-Mcan/iptv-player) to review, use and
improve for non-commercial purposes, and contributions are welcome. There is no installer yet: you
build it from the code, with .NET 8.
