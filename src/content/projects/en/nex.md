## Context

Music fandoms have enormous energy: they want to take part, vote, compete and hear their group on
air. Traditional radio leaves no room for that.

## What it is

An interactive radio station in real time. The programming is decided by the audience, not an
editor: while you listen, you use your influence to choose what plays next, join the chat and
events, and see the result right away.

A meeting point for fandoms and a step forward for radio entertainment.

## What I did

Everything: the product, the architecture, the database, the integration with the streaming server,
the media and audio API, the web app and the native app for iOS and Android.

## Status

Getting ready for launch. At [nex-radio.com](https://nex-radio.com) you can already listen without
